1. Who is responsible

Hannes-Florin Horn, c/o Berliner Ruder-Club, Bismarckstr. 4, 14109 Berlin, Germany. Email: hannesflorin@gmail.com. This is a private project; it is not operated by the Berliner Ruder-Club.

2. What data is stored

  • Account: your name and email address. Your password is stored only as a secure hash, never in readable form.
  • Training data: the date of each session, the exercises, weights, repetitions and when a session was started and finished.
  • Technical data: when the app runs on a server, the server may briefly log your IP address and the pages requested, to keep the service running and secure.

3. Why, and on what legal basis

The data is used only to let you record your training and review your progress, and to let the coach follow the group's training. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given when your account is created. You can withdraw your consent at any time by email; your data is then deleted. Withdrawing does not affect the lawfulness of the processing before it.

4. Who can see your data

  • You: your own data only.
  • The coach, with a separate staff account: the training data of all athletes, read only.
  • The operator named above, as administrator of the app.

Your data is not sold or passed on to third parties, and it is not used for advertising.

5. Cookies

The app sets only the cookies it needs to work: one that keeps you logged in, and one that protects forms against misuse (CSRF protection). There are no tracking or advertising cookies. Fonts and styles are served by the app itself; no data is sent to Google or other third parties when a page loads.

6. Hosting

The app will be hosted by a provider in Germany, who processes the data only on the operator's behalf. This notice will name the provider once the app is online.

7. How long data is kept

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. It is deleted when you ask for it, and at the latest when you leave the training group. A login expires after a period without activity (7 days for athletes, 30 minutes for staff accounts).

8. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict its processing, to receive it in a common format, to object to its processing and to withdraw your consent. Send an email to the address above.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, for example the one responsible for the operator: Die Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und für das Recht auf Akteneinsicht Brandenburg (LDA Brandenburg).
